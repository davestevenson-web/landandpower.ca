About

The early stages of energy project development.

Land and Power is an Ontario company focused on enabling energy project development, with data centres as the lead focus. The company grew out of hands-on experience delivering major capital projects, including project management, large-scale permitting, and regulatory compliance and reporting.

Our screening, research, and diligence are conducted using systems we developed in-house. This allows the company to operate with a small team, low overhead, and timely decision-making.

We are not developers or operators. We work where land, regulation, and infrastructure meet: doing the early, complex work that lets energy projects move faster and with more certainty.

Every engagement starts with the landowner: a fair, transparent process that protects their interests and works to realize the full value of their land.

  • Based in Southwestern Ontario
  • Working across Canada
  • Site origination focused in Ontario and Alberta
  • Focused on energy project development
