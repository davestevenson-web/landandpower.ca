What we do

The groundwork behind every data center.

Before construction begins, a data center needs its land secured, its permits advanced, and its power arranged. That early, complex work is what we do.

Site identification & land partnerships

We screen land for data center development — location, servicing, permitting risk, and community fit — and work directly with landowners to bring the strongest sites to market. Their interests come first: a fair, transparent process that protects them and realizes the full value of their land.

Permitting & approvals

We guide projects through municipal, provincial, and federal permitting and regulatory approvals, from early screening and pre-consultation through to final approval.

Power & utility coordination

Power is the defining constraint on modern data centers. We engage early with utilities and system operators on servicing, grid connection, and infrastructure planning.

Development support

We manage the moving pieces that turn a promising parcel into a build-ready site: studies, consultants, agencies, and timelines.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you build or operate data centers?

No. Companies that assemble capital and infrastructure to build and run sites are developers. We're the step before that. Our work happens before construction: securing land, advancing permits and approvals, and coordinating power and infrastructure. Developers and operators inherit a site that's ready to build on, and then we step aside.

Who do you typically work with?

It starts with landowners evaluating their property's potential. We also work with developers and operators looking for de-risked sites, and the utilities and agencies we coordinate with on every project.

Where do you operate?

We are an Ontario-based company, focused on data center development here, and we take on projects across Canada.

When is the right time to talk?

As early as possible. Securing land, advancing permits, and arranging power takes time, and none of it can be rushed at the end. Starting early avoids delays and takes uncertainty out of the schedule.

What does working with you look like?

It starts with a conversation. Every site and project is different, so we scope our involvement around what the project actually needs, from early screening to full development support.

What about community acceptance and environmental requirements?

They're central to the work. A data center has to earn its place in its community, and both federal and Ontario guidance expect projects to deliver real local benefits. So we do this work up front: listening before plans are fixed, working environmental requirements into the design instead of bolting them on later, and making the local upside concrete and visible.
