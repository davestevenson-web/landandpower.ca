Developers and Investors

For developers and investors.

Origination

Sites, Powered and Permitted

Screened sites with the permitting groundwork done, the stakeholders engaged, and the power picture clear. Origination runs in Ontario and Alberta, where the screening work is deepest.

Independent diligence

A fixed-scope diligence sprint on a single site or a shortlist: permitting risk, power availability, servicing, and timeline, reported against the same gates we apply to our own pipeline. The read is independent. If the evidence does not support the site, the report says so.

Already have your site?

We help proponents set the permitting position before the final design is fixed, so approvals are not waiting on procurement decisions.

A clean handoff

When we deliver a site, the development team receives the full record of the work behind it, including the screening results, any constraints on the land, and the approvals still outstanding. The team can then price the project and plan its next steps with the complete picture in front of it.
