FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do you build or operate energy projects?

No. Companies that assemble capital and infrastructure to build and run sites are developers. We are the step before that. Our work happens before construction: securing land, advancing permits and approvals, and coordinating power and infrastructure. Developers and operators inherit a site that is ready to build on.

Who do you typically work with?

It starts with landowners evaluating the potential of their land. We also work with developers and operators looking for de-risked sites, municipalities preparing for large-load applications, and the utilities and agencies we coordinate with on every project.

Where do you operate?

Site origination runs in Ontario and Alberta. Advisory and diligence work is available across Canada. The company is based in Southwestern Ontario.

When is the right time to talk?

As early as possible. Securing land, advancing permits, and arranging power takes time, and none of it can be rushed at the end. Starting early reduces the risk of delays and takes uncertainty out of the schedule.

What does working with you look like?

It starts with a conversation. Every site and project is different, so we scope our involvement around what the project actually needs, from early screening to full development support, or standalone diligence and advisory.

What about community acceptance and environmental requirements?

They are central to the work. A project has to earn its place in its community, and both federal and Ontario guidance expect projects to deliver real local benefits. So we do this work up front: listening before plans are fixed, working environmental requirements into the design instead of bolting them on later, and designing every project so the benefits to the community outweigh the impacts.

Do you advise municipalities and project proponents in the same community?

No. Land and Power works on one side in any municipality. A municipality the company advises is a municipality where the company holds no project position.
