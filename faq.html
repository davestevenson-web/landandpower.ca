FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Do you build or operate energy projects?

No. Companies that assemble capital and infrastructure to build and run sites are developers. We are the step before that. Our work happens before construction: securing land, advancing permits and approvals, and coordinating power and infrastructure. Developers and operators inherit a site that is ready to build on.

Who do you typically work with?

We work with landowners evaluating the potential of their land, developers and operators seeking de-risked sites, municipalities preparing for large-load applications, and utilities and agencies.

Where do you operate?

Our site origination work is focused in Ontario and Alberta. Our advisory and diligence services are available across Canada. The company is based in Southwestern Ontario.

When is the right time to talk?

As early as possible. Securing land, advancing permits, and arranging power takes time, and none of it can be rushed at the end. Starting early reduces the risk of delays and takes uncertainty out of the schedule.

What does working with you look like?

It starts with a conversation. Every site and project is different, so we scope our involvement around what the project actually needs, from early screening to full development support, or standalone diligence and advisory.

What about community acceptance and environmental requirements?

This is central to our work. We address community acceptance and environmental requirements from the outset: engaging with the community before plans are finalized, incorporating environmental requirements into the design rather than adding them afterward, and structuring each project so its benefits to the community outweigh its impacts. Ontario's Data Centre Playbook and the federal Responsible Data Centre Development Principles both expect projects to deliver real local benefits, and our process is built to meet that expectation.

Do you advise municipalities and project proponents in the same community?

No. Land and Power acts for one party only in any municipality, and does not advise a municipality in which it holds a project position.
