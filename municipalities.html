Municipalities

For municipalities.

Data centres and other large loads are arriving in communities that have no local rules for them yet. The first application should not be the moment a municipality has to improvise.

Data centre readiness framework

A fixed-scope advisory engagement that gives council and staff a working framework before the first application lands:

  • Alignment with the Ontario Data Centre Playbook and the federal development principles
  • A power and servicing screen for the community
  • A full cost-recovery model, so local taxpayers and ratepayers do not subsidize a connection
  • A community-benefit framework template
  • An application checklist and process map that applicants and residents can both read

Advice, not advocacy

The framework is advisory. Every decision stays with council and staff. Land and Power advises a municipality only where the company holds no project position of its own: one side per municipality, on the record.

Timing

The framework is fastest before an application forces the pace. A municipality that has its rules in place before an application arrives is in a stronger position when it reviews that application.
